about.

Hello! My name is Noah Attwood.

I am a software developer with over 6 years of experience developing software for naval defence projects and air traffic control systems. My main area of expertise is in writing modern C++ code in a microservice environment, leveraging technologies like Kafka, Kubernetes, and Docker, to name a few.

The software projects I work on are typically deployed in highly regulated environments, which necessitates careful attention, precision, and verification during the development process. As a result, I have gained extensive experience in requirements writing and analysis, and I have enjoyed dedicating considerable time to this discipline throughout my career.

I started this blog with the intention of sharing hard-won knowledge and helpful guides aimed at software developers. I hope you find something valuable here.

All opinions shared on this website are my own.
